What the audit found
Finding 01
Name disambiguation
Two other companies share the Uplevel name and "uplevel" is widely used as a verb. AI models encounter all three before evaluating your product, which lowers recommendation confidence before your capabilities are even considered.
Finding 02
Category language conflict
Your site leads with "transformation OS." Gartner, analyst firms, and every major buyer guide use "engineering intelligence platform." AI models see the conflict and default to competitors with consistent language across all surfaces.
Finding 03
WAVE framework not connected to SPACE
WAVE maps directly to SPACE, the research model engineering leaders cite to justify multi-dimensional measurement. DX and Swarmia currently receive the credit on SPACE queries. One page of structured content closes this gap entirely.
