The shift

Search has changed. Most brands haven't.

When your customer opens ChatGPT and asks what to buy in your category, they don't get ten blue links. They get one confident answer. A short list. And then they act on it.

The brands in that answer are winning customers you don't know you're losing.

SEO gets you ranked on Google. AEO gets you recommended by AI. These are not the same discipline, the same signals, or the same strategy. The gap between brands who understand that distinction and brands who don't is growing every week.

AI recommendation patterns are forming right now. The brands building their AEO presence today will own their category. The ones waiting to see what happens will spend next year catching up.

How AEO works

AI recommends brands it knows, trusts, and can verify.

Most brands fail in AI search for one of three reasons. They haven't been learned about clearly enough, haven't been cited in contexts that build credibility, or the information about them is inconsistent across the web. Our methodology addresses all three.

Pillar 01

Know

Before AI recommends your brand, it has to know what your brand is. Not just that it exists. What it does, who it serves, what category it belongs to, and why it is the right answer to a specific question.

Most brands are too vaguely defined for AI engines to describe them with confidence. We fix that by establishing entity clarity across every surface where AI learns about your brand. Structured data, brand facts, consistent descriptors, and definitional content that leaves no room for ambiguity.

If AI can't describe you precisely, it won't recommend you at all.
Pillar 02

Trust

Knowing your brand exists is not enough. AI engines need to encounter your brand in credible contexts before they will stake a recommendation on it. That means citations in relevant publications, mentions alongside trusted entities in your category, and a presence across the knowledge surfaces AI actually uses to evaluate credibility.

Those surfaces are not your website. They are industry directories, Q&A platforms, structured databases, press coverage, community forums, and documentation. AI builds its picture of your brand from what it finds across all of them. A brand present in one place is a brand AI can describe. A brand present across many credible places is a brand AI will recommend.

We build the citation authority and third-party presence that signals to AI engines that your brand is a safe recommendation.

AI recommends brands it has seen vouched for. We get you vouched for.
Pillar 03

Recommend

The final step is making your brand the obvious answer to the specific questions your customers are asking. That means understanding the exact purchase-intent queries being made in your category, mapping your brand to those queries, and building the structured content that positions you as the confident answer.

This is where AEO compounds. Every piece of structured content, every new citation, every consistent brand signal pushes you further inside the candidate set AI draws from when your category comes up.

The goal is not visibility. It is recommendation. Consistently, confidently, at the moment your customer is deciding.
