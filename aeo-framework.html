The three-sentence explanation

For 20 years, the goal was page one of Google. That was a list of ten options. AI search doesn't give people a list — it gives them an answer. One brand. Maybe three. If that's not you, you don't exist in that moment. AEO is how we make sure it's you.

01 / Presence

Does AI know you exist?

Can AI engines find your brand when someone asks about your category? Presence is the baseline. Without it, nothing else matters.

02 / Trust

Does AI trust what it finds?

When AI finds you, does it have structured, verified, consistent information to feel confident recommending you? Inconsistency kills recommendations.

03 / Authority

Do third parties back you?

AI chooses brands that other sources talk about. Third-party citations, reviews, and earned media are the signals that tip the recommendation.

04 / Momentum

Are you compounding?

AI models update. Competitors catch on. The brands that maintain and compound their position are the ones that stay recommended.

The mental model

A flywheel,
not a funnel

There's a foundational sequence: you can't earn AI citations before you've built the infrastructure for AI to cite. You can't maintain a position you haven't earned.

But the accurate mental model is a flywheel. Each cycle tightens. Each Answer Hub update increases citation probability. Each new third-party mention raises AI confidence. Each monthly audit reveals new gaps and new wins.

The brands that dominate AI recommendations aren't the ones who built it once. They're the ones who kept turning the flywheel.

Common questions

AEO explained

AEO is the practice of making a brand findable, trustworthy, and recommendable by AI engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. Unlike SEO which targets ranked lists, AEO targets single-answer recommendations. When someone asks an AI "what's the best supplement for sleep?" your brand is either in the answer, or it isn't. There's no page two.

SEO targets page-one rankings in a list of ten blue links. AEO targets direct recommendations where AI gives one answer, sometimes three. The mechanics are different too: SEO rewards backlinks and keyword density. AEO rewards structured data, entity consistency, third-party citations, and the quality of information available about your brand across the entire web.

The Edge Studio framework structures the first 90 days as an Infrastructure Sprint, followed by an Authority Sprint, followed by Momentum. Most brands see measurable AI visibility improvement within 60 days of implementing the foundation layers. The compounding effect typically becomes significant from month three onward.

An AI Visibility Score measures how many of a brand's highest purchase-intent queries result in an AI recommendation. Edge Studio's Found By AI tool runs 20 purchase-intent prompts across AI engines and scores brands out of 20. It's the core metric we track because it directly reflects the commercial impact of AEO work.

No, and any agency telling you to abandon SEO entirely is wrong. The two disciplines reinforce each other. Strong domain authority from SEO creates a foundation AI trusts. Strong AEO signals (structured data, entity consistency) improve how search engines understand and categorise your brand. The brands building both in parallel are the ones that will own their category in both search and AI by 2027.
