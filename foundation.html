The Eligibility Layer

AI systems decide which brands they trust enough to recommend before any selection happens. Most brands are in AI's knowledge base. Almost none have a strategy for being in its recommendation set.

01 / First condition

Understood

Binary · Fixable

AI can accurately describe your brand — what it is, what it does, who it serves — without ambiguity or contradiction. Entity clarity, consistent facts, machine-readable structure.

  • Entity clarity across every surface AI learns from
  • Consistent facts — same name, category, positioning everywhere
  • Machine-readable structure AI can parse and cite

02 / Second condition

Trusted

Threshold + gradient · Buildable

AI has encountered your brand in credible, relevant contexts enough times to include it in the candidate set for recommendation. This is Machine-Readable Reputation.

  • Third-party citations in relevant publications and sources
  • Co-occurrence with trusted entities in your category
  • Factual consistency — no contradictions across sources

03 / Third condition

Preferred

Gradient · Competitive · Ongoing

When two trusted brands compete for the same recommendation, AI reaches for yours first. Category share of voice, authority amplification, PR at scale.

  • Category share of voice across credible sources
  • PR and authority amplification at scale
  • Founder positioning as a named category expert
